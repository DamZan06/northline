ONE COUNTRY. ONE DIRECTION.

The second NorthLine. One athlete, no support car.

The journey begins in Bargen and ends in Chiasso: around 300 kilometres from north to south, undertaken alone and without vehicle support.

WHY NorthLine 2.0?

The horizon is always ahead.

We never reach it by staring at it. We reach it by moving toward it. NorthLine 2.0 is the idea of continuing forward as a team, even when the destination is still far away. One kilometre becomes the next. One step becomes another. Until the horizon becomes the finish line.

THE CHALLENGE

What is NorthLine 2.0?

NorthLine 2.0 crosses Switzerland on foot from Bargen to Chiasso. It is the second time Damiano faces this challenge, now alone and without a support car.

The expedition is solo and human-powered. There is no support car: equipment, navigation, fatigue and unexpected problems must all be managed independently.

NorthLine 2.0 exists to be followed in real time: the website is the public record of where we are, how much is complete and what the journey demands from day to day.
