ABOUT NorthLine 2.0

Second attempt. One person. No support car.

NorthLine 2.0 is Damiano's second crossing of Switzerland: from Bargen to Chiasso, alone and without a support car.

What NorthLine 2.0 is

NorthLine 2.0 is a human-powered crossing of roughly 300 km from north to south, with around 7,000 m of positive elevation gain.

Motivation

Having already completed NorthLine once, Damiano returns to the challenge alone. Every pacing decision, difficult kilometre and recovery is now his responsibility.

Preparation

Preparation includes training, route study, gear selection and logistical planning. The platform and tools used to share the journey were developed to prioritize reliability and minimalism.

Equipment

Lightweight but safe equipment is essential. GPS, emergency gear, batteries and technical clothing are balanced to ensure autonomy and safety across variable alpine terrain and weather.

Nutrition

Sustaining long hours of movement requires careful energy and hydration management: water, electrolytes and high-energy food are consumed to maintain performance and recover between days.

Route

The route crosses lakes, valleys, forests and alpine passes. North is up: the route follows the actual GPX and is not altered for visual reasons.

No support car

There will be no support car or travelling support team. Equipment, food, navigation and unforeseen problems must be managed independently throughout the route.
